This policy covers the text message program for Drue Inman's assistant line, (213) 369-0221, run by Drue Inman, an individual AV integrator in Texas.

What the program is

The line sends and receives texts about scheduling and job updates with Drue Inman and with people who have opted in. No marketing messages are sent.

How you opt in

You opt in only by sending your own text message (for example START) to (213) 369-0221. You then get a confirmation text. No one is texted who has not texted the line first. No purchased or rented lists are used.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

HELP and STOP

Reply HELP for help, or email drueinman@gmail.com. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; you will get one confirmation and no more texts. Reply START to opt back in.

What information is collected and how it is used

The program keeps your mobile number, your opt-in and opt-out status, and the messages exchanged, only to run the conversation and keep a record of consent.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your number and messages are never sold. Information is passed only to the phone carrier and messaging provider needed to deliver texts, or when required by law.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy: drueinman@gmail.com. Assistant line: (213) 369-0221.
