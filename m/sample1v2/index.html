Aloy

“· Eve” demos use xAI's Eve voice (prerendered through Telnyx, lips from Rhubarb). The others use Kokoro af_heart, a free stand-in. Tap one to pick it, then Playback.

Big peaks (laugh, flash, gasp) never play while she talks: they wait for the next comma or period and play as their own beat. Talking faces change her eyes and brows while her lips keep moving.

100%

All subtle at 100%: drift is a smoothed random walk (about 0.7% move, 0.3° turn), breathing is about 0.5% over an uneven 4 to 5 seconds, and micro-expressions are faint, eased looks at random times. Blinks always stay on.

Phone voice (stand-in) · how cues work

Typed text uses your phone's built-in voice, a stand-in for Aloy's real voice. Tags: [laugh] [excited] [shock] [gasp] are big peaks (they wait for the next comma or period). [disbelief] [think] [skeptical] [confused] [worried] [sorry] [relieved] [proud] [determined] [focused] [listening] [sleepy] change her look while she keeps talking; [smile] goes back.
