Aloy
PC mode: the desktop layout comes later. For now it's the phone layout.

Demos are in Aloy's voice (Eve), prerendered, with lips from Rhubarb. Tap one to pick it, then Playback.

Big peaks (laugh, flash, gasp) never play while she talks: they wait for the next comma or period and play as their own beat. Talking faces change her eyes and brows while her lips keep moving.

100%

All subtle at 100%: drift is a smoothed random walk (about 0.7% move, 0.3° turn), breathing is about 0.5% over an uneven 4 to 5 seconds, and micro-expressions are faint, eased looks at random times. Blinks always stay on.

Phone layout. It's picked automatically (phones and narrow screens use Phone, wide screens use PC), and your choice is remembered on this device.

How cues work

Go closes this sheet, waits a moment, then she says it in her own voice. Tags: [laugh] [excited] [shock] [gasp] are big peaks (they wait for the next comma or period). [disbelief] [think] [skeptical] [confused] [worried] [sorry] [relieved] [proud] [determined] [focused] [listening] [sleepy] [sad] [tears] [lookdown] [guilty] [lookup] [eyeroll] change her look while she keeps talking; [smile] goes back. Cues can sit inside a word, like chord marks; the chart above shows where each lands.
